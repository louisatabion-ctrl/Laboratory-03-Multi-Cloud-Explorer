# Mission Reflection

Completing Laboratory 03 – Multi-Cloud Explorer gave me a deeper understanding of how cloud platforms differ and how business requirements drive technical decisions. Among the three providers, I was most impressed by **Google Cloud Platform (GCP)** because of its leadership in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning. Its integration with TensorFlow and BigQuery makes it a powerful choice for research institutions and data-driven companies. However, AWS and Azure also stood out in their own ways, with AWS offering unmatched breadth of services and Azure providing seamless integration with Microsoft technologies.

Despite their differences, AWS, Azure, and GCP share many similarities. All three offer core services in compute, storage, networking, and identity management. They also provide global infrastructure with multiple regions and availability zones, ensuring high availability and reliability. Each platform emphasizes scalability, security, and flexibility, which are essential for modern enterprises.

Business requirements strongly influence cloud platform selection. For example, a startup with limited budget benefits from AWS’s free tier and scalability, while a university already using Microsoft 365 and Active Directory finds Azure the most practical choice. Similarly, an AI research company would gain the most value from GCP’s advanced ML tools. This exercise highlighted that choosing a cloud provider is not about popularity but about aligning technical capabilities with organizational needs.

Acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant taught me to think critically about matching services to scenarios. I learned to evaluate platforms not only by their features but also by how they solve specific business problems. My GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio improved significantly through this mission, as I added structured documentation, comparison tables, client recommendations, and screenshots. The portfolio now reflects professional technical writing and organized evidence, making it a valuable resource for future projects and presentations.
